## 📚 Practice Questions

1. Show the total number of employees in each department whose salary is greater than or equal to 50,000.
2. Show the average salary of employees in each city whose age is 25 years or above.
3. Show the total salary paid in each department for employees whose salary is greater than 45,000.
4. Show the total number of employees in each city who work in the IT department.
5. Show the minimum salary in each city for employees whose age is less than 30 years.
6. Show the total salary paid in each city for employees whose age is greater than 25 years.
7. Show the total number of employees in each department whose city is 'Delhi'.
8. Show the highest salary in each department for employees whose age is between 25 and 30 years.
9. Show the average salary in each department for employees whose age is between 24 and 32 years.
10. Show the total number of employees in each department whose city is either 'Delhi' or 'Kanpur'.
